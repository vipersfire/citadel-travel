## Remaining inputs before production

- Approved real photography/video, final bilingual package/legal copy, real contact details and usage rights.
- Google OAuth production registration and a real-account sign-in/link check.
- Hosted PostgreSQL connectivity, R2 public-media upload, local private-file volume backup/restore, backup/PITR policy and provider-specific smoke checks.
- Xendit staging callbacks and channel approval, then production merchant activation; Resend sender verification; approved WhatsApp templates if delivery is enabled.
- Domain/CDN/proxy configuration, monitored release and rollback exercise, and observed uptime.
- Human business UAT, screen-reader/device review, and authenticated staging security review.

The development environment remains usable without WhatsApp/Xendit credentials. The production stages above are configuration and acceptance gates, not missing local-development keys.
