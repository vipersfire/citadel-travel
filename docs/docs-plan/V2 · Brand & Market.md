## Owner direction on luxury (23 October 2026)

Luxury is not a product line and is not the same kind of thing as sport. It describes the **design**: look, feel, UI and UX. The business is sport tourism only. The V2 brief mentions a secondary Luxury collection and two Luxury products. This spec drops both. Home section 5 is “Beyond the event” (cities, food, companion activities) attached to sport trips, and the pilot goal is two **sport** products ready to sell. The word “luxury” does not appear in public copy.

## Positioning

| Element | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Parent brand | **Citadel Tour Travel**. “Sports Travel” is the descriptor of the product line. No legal or company name change. |
| Descriptor | European Sports Experiences for Indonesian Fans |
| Tagline | **Be There When It Matters.** |
| Promise | Journeys designed around the sporting moments that matter to their fans. |
| Vision | Opening the way for Indonesian fans to experience the world’s sporting stages. |
| First sales focus | Europe, and only events and ticket categories that can actually be supplied. |

“Pioneer” is an aspiration, never a claim to be first. A specialist reputation is built through product depth, proof of service and guest experience.

## Why this position

- An Indonesian operator, Golden Rama, already lists a Sport Holidays category ([S1](https://goldenrama.com/)). So “sport tourism” alone does not differentiate Citadel. The positioning has to be more specific.
- SportsBreaks ties fan emotion to clear tickets and travel ([S2](https://www.sportsbreaks.com/About-Us)). It is a communication benchmark.
- Grand Slam Tennis Tours pairs tennis specialism with a holiday experience ([S3](https://www.grandslamtennistours.com/traveling-with-us/gstt-experience)). It is a product-depth benchmark, not evidence of Citadel sales.

These show that such offers exist. They do not show demand or market share.

## Market hypotheses to test

| Starting segment | What must be proven |
| --- | --- |
| Football and motorsport fans | Willingness to pay for ticket category, venue coordination and a clear schedule. |
| Tennis-watching couples or families | Value of combining match sessions, companion activities and trip pace. |
| Runners and companions | Certainty of race-entry rights, race-week needs and the value of a companion itinerary. |

These are product hypotheses, not survey results or market-size estimates.

**Validation plan (a method, not research already done):**

1. Interview **12** prospective buyers across the three segments (4 each). Record objections, budget, timing and the products they actually ask for. Twelve is a plan, not a representative sample.
2. Run a comprehension test with **5** prospective guests on the prototype. Ask: what does Citadel do, which event interests you, what is included, what happens if the schedule changes. Record task completion and doubts.
3. Persona assumptions (age 30–55, home city, halal needs, companion language, group size) come from team documents. Confirm them in the interviews.

## Claims policy

| Allowed | Not allowed without proof |
| --- | --- |
| Aspirational lines (“opening the way for Indonesian fans”). | “First”, “pioneer” as a fact. |
| Describing what each personal offer contains. | “Official partner” of any club, event or organizer. |
| Event schedule, Indonesian-speaking companion, halal options and seats together, explained per offer. | Promised VIP access, specific seats, 24-hour service, or any of the above as a site-wide promise. |

The CMS blocks publishing text that contains these terms unless an admin gives a reason (FR-94).

## Product hierarchy and publishing

Keep the six concepts OT, EC, RG, WB, F1, BM as the **draft** catalog. A product is published as **ready to offer** only after its supplier and service coverage are verified. Other concepts may appear as **interest collection** with a clear label (“Kami sedang menyiapkan perjalanan ini / We are preparing this trip”) and an interest form only. Because the catalog is sport only, nothing is added to “make up numbers”.

| Readiness | Public display | What a visitor can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `draft` | Not shown | Nothing |
| `interest_only` | Shown with the “preparing” label | Leave interest (no booking request) |
| `ready_to_offer` | Shown as a bookable product | Discuss the trip, or request it with a verified account |

## Pricing and enquiry quality

No public prices (kept from baseline). To compensate, each product page states the known duration or night range, hotel type, event coverage and exclusions. The first form asks only for name, WhatsApp number, travel interest and number of travelers. Preferred time, email and budget are optional. **No passport at this stage.**

## Four certainties kept separate

A “confirmed” on one dimension does not prove the others.

| Fact | Question it answers | Stored in | Visible to |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Event schedule | When does the event take place? | `events.date_status`, `events.date_verified_at` | Public and members |
| Ticket allocation | What access rights are actually available? | `batches.ticket_allocation_status`, event-ticket services | Public (open batches) and members |
| Departure | Is the trip guaranteed to run? | `batches.status` (`confirmed`), `departure_confirmed_at` | Public (open batches) and members |
| Payment | Has the invoice been received? | `invoices.status`, `payments` | Members only |

## Tickets via official apps

The portal keeps status, access category, instructions and the handover target for tickets. A ticket that must be delivered through an official app is **not** forced into a PDF. The Wimbledon 2025 handbook is an example of access by court and date rather than by named player ([S4](https://www.wimbledon.com/pdf/2025_Debenture_Handbook.pdf)). Conditions for the season being sold must be verified again.

## Success measures for the first 30 days

- Measure: product visits, successful enquiries, qualified leads, offers issued, valid payments.
- **Qualified lead:** event interest, number of travelers, date range and a contact the team can act on. Budget is recorded if offered. A WhatsApp click is a click, not a lead or a booking.
- First human response: **at most one business day**. Offer issue time is reported separately, because it depends on the supplier.
- Conversion is reported as numerator and denominator per stage. Registered users and buyers are never mixed.
